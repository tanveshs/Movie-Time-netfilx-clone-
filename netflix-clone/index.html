Frequently Asked Questions

What is MovieTime?

MovieTime is a streaming service that offers a wide variety of award-winning TV shows, movies, anime, documentaries, and more on thousands of internet-connected devices.

You can watch as much as you want, whenever you want without a single commercial – all for one low monthly price. There's always something new to discover and new movies are added every week!

How much does MovieTime cost?

Watch MovieTime on your smartphone, tablet, Smart TV, laptop, or streaming device, all for one fixed monthly fee. Plans range from $6.99 to $22.99 a month. No extra costs, no contracts.

Where can I watch?

Watch anywhere, anytime. Sign in with your MovieTime account to watch instantly on the web from your personal computer or on any internet-connected device that offers the MovieTime app.

You can also download your favorite shows with the iOS or Android app to watch offline without an internet connection.

How do I cancel?

MovieTime is flexible. There are no contracts and no commitments. You can easily cancel your account online in two clicks without cancellation fees.

What can I watch on MovieTime?

MovieTime has an extensive library of feature films, documentaries, TV shows, anime, award-winning originals, and more.
